# Interviews: Client Guidelines

> What we need from a client for one feature. Applies across all our sites. Text copied automatically from the [web page](https://a2b-global-media-content-team.github.io/client-resources/interview-guidelines.html); that page is the master version.

Interviews

## Your story, in your *words*

Interviews give our writers your expertise first-hand. There are two kinds: interviews for your content writing articles, and event interviews on your stand.

## Interviews for Your Articles

If your package includes content writing, each article can start from an interview with you or one of your experts. Your content writer arranges the interview with you.

- Choose a spokesperson who knows the topic well
- Agree the topic with your writer beforehand
- Share any background material before the interview
- Have images and links ready for the article

Interviews are usually held online, by video call. Sometimes we offer them in person at events.

## Event Interviews

Available as an additional service: we come to your stand at an event, interview your spokesperson in person, and leave with both the words and the pictures.

**Up to 1,000 words** Article

**Photography** Taken at the event

- The event, your stand number and hall
- Your spokesperson and the time they are available
- The topics or products you want to cover

## Before the Interview

When booking

#### Agree the topic

Tell us the topic and who we will speak to.

Before the interview

#### Share background

Send us any background material, such as technical papers or product details.

After the interview

#### Draft for you to check

Your writer turns the interview into an article and sends you the draft to check.

After your check

#### Published

We publish the article on our site and include it in our newsletter.
