# Visual Guide: Future Transport-News

> Where each package feature appears on the site, with real client examples. Text copied automatically from the [web page](https://a2b-global-media-content-team.github.io/client-resources/FTN-visual-guide.html); that page is the master version.

Take a guided tour of Future Transport-News and see exactly where each part of your package appears.

## 1. Homepage

Example shown: [futuretransport-news.com](https://futuretransport-news.com)

Watch this part of the tour: https://a2b-global-media-content-team.github.io/client-resources/FTN-visual-guide.html#homepage

**Homepage featured supplier position**: A banner advert in the Featured Suppliers section of our homepage, linking to your website. Depending on your package, it shows for 4 weeks or for your whole contract.

**Format**: JPEG, PNG or GIF, 1024 × 512 px.

**Latest News banner**: A second banner position in the Latest News grid near the top of the homepage, linking to your website.

**Your news and videos on the homepage**: Your press releases appear in the Supplier News section, and your videos in Video Highlights.

**Design service**: We can design your homepage banner for you. Ask us for details.

### Tour steps

1. **The Future Transport-News homepage**: The homepage is the busiest page on Future Transport-News, with the latest future transport news, events and videos.

2. **Banner in the latest news**: A banner position sits in the Latest News grid near the top of the homepage, right beside the newest headlines, with a link to your website. Until a client books it, it shows our own advert.

3. **Your news on the homepage**: Your press releases appear in the Supplier News section of the homepage, as well as on your profile.

4. **Featured Suppliers**: The homepage featured supplier position puts your banner advert in the Featured Suppliers section, with a link to your website. Depending on your package, it shows for 4 weeks or for your whole contract. (1024 × 512 px; JPEG, PNG or GIF)

5. **Your videos on the homepage**: Every video you send us features in Video Highlights on the homepage.

## 2. Company Profile

Example shown: [futuretransport-news.com/suppliers/geotab-fleet-management](https://futuretransport-news.com/suppliers/geotab-fleet-management)

Watch this part of the tour: https://a2b-global-media-content-team.github.io/client-resources/FTN-visual-guide.html#company-profile

**Hero image**: A showcase image behind your company name.

**Profile text**: 300+ words, written with key phrases that help search engines send traffic to your page.

**Logo, links and contact details**: Your logo, website and social media links, address and telephone number.

**Events**: The events you are attending, with dates and stand numbers. Your next event also shows beside your enquiry form.

**News, videos, documents and images**: Your own news feed, video gallery, downloadable PDFs and image gallery. Add new content at any time.

**Enquiry form**: Readers can contact you directly from your profile.

### Tour steps

1. **Your company profile**: Your profile is your home on Future Transport-News. It brings together everything about your company in one place, and it is where readers land from your news, banners and listings.

2. **Hero image**: A large showcase image runs behind your company name at the top of the page. Choose an image that shows your products or people at their best. (Landscape image; High resolution)

3. **Logo, links and contact details**: Your logo sits beside your profile, with links to your website and social media, your address and a telephone button. Readers can also save you as a favourite.

4. **Profile text**: 300+ words about your company, enriched with key phrases so search engines send relevant traffic to your profile. You can add your products and services too. (300+ words)

5. **Latest news, right beside your profile**: Your most recent news items show in the sidebar, with a link to all your supplier news.

6. **Video gallery**: Host your videos on your profile and add new ones at any time. Every video you send us also features on our homepage and in our newsletter.

7. **News feed**: Every press release you send us appears in your own news feed. Your news also features on the homepage, in our newsletter and on our LinkedIn page.

8. **Documents**: Host brochures, case studies and white papers as PDFs on your profile. Each document is optimised for search engines, so buyers can find it. (PDF)

9. **Image gallery**: Add as many images as you like. Captions help search engines find your images. (Unlimited images)

10. **Events and enquiry form**: Readers can contact you directly with the enquiry form at the bottom of your profile. Enquiries go straight to your inbox. When you add the events you are attending, your next event and stand number show beside the form too. (Event dates; Stand numbers)

## 4. Digital Newsletter

Example shown: [mailchi.mp/futuretransport-news.com/future-transport-news-week-43-2026](https://mailchi.mp/futuretransport-news.com/future-transport-news-week-43-2026)

Watch this part of the tour: https://a2b-global-media-content-team.github.io/client-resources/FTN-visual-guide.html#digital-newsletter

**Your news in our newsletter**: Your latest news listed in our newsletter, with a link to the full article on Future Transport-News. Maximum one item per newsletter.

### Tour steps

1. **Our digital newsletter**: Our weekly newsletter goes out every Wednesday, with the latest future transport news and news from our suppliers.

2. **Your news in our newsletter**: Your latest news is listed under “Latest Supplier News”, with a link to the full article on Future Transport-News. Maximum one item per newsletter.

3. **Newsletter banners**: A banner in our newsletter with a link to your website. We use your Products & Services banner, unless you send us a different one. (300 × 250 px; JPEG, PNG or GIF)

4. **Upcoming events**: On a Premium or Ultra package, your own events and webinars can appear in the Upcoming Events section of the newsletter.

## 5. Targeted Mail

Watch this part of the tour: https://a2b-global-media-content-team.github.io/client-resources/FTN-visual-guide.html#targeted-mail

An email with only your content, sent to your most relevant audience, chosen by our AI software, with optional parameters (for example, region) from you.

**Process**: Send your content one week before the agreed send date. We send you a preview to approve, then send your email at the agreed date and time.

**We need**: A subject line (about 65 characters), the content (text and images, as PDF or HTML), valid links, and your preferred audience parameters.

**Design service**: We can design your campaign for you. Ask us for details.

### Tour steps

1. **AI-targeted mail**: An email campaign with only your content: press releases, videos, brochures, webinars or event invitations. This example is for a made-up company.

2. **Sent by Future Transport-News**: Your email is sent from Future Transport-News, a sender our readers know and trust, which helps it reach the inbox and get opened.

3. **Your content, your audience**: Your mail goes to your most relevant audience, chosen by our AI software, with optional parameters (for example, region) from you.

4. **Your call to action**: Each mail leads to a clear next step, such as a link to your website, a demo request or your stand at an event.

5. **How it works**: - Send your content one week before the agreed send date
- We send you a preview to approve
- We send your email at the agreed date and time (Subject line ≈ 65 characters; PDF or HTML; Valid links)
