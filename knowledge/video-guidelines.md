# Videos: Client Guidelines

> What we need from a client for one feature. Applies across all our sites. Text copied automatically from the [web page](https://a2b-global-media-content-team.github.io/client-resources/video-guidelines.html); that page is the master version.

Videos

## Your videos, on our *site*

Every video you send us features on your company profile, our homepage and our newsletter.

## What You Get

- In the video gallery on your company profile
- In the video section of our homepage
- In our newsletter, as the Featured Video

Newsletter space is shared fairly between clients. If you send several videos at once, we spread them across newsletter issues rather than including them all in one.

**Not on social media**

We feature your videos on your profile, our homepage and our newsletter. We do not post them on our social media.

## What We Need From You

- A link to each video: we prefer **YouTube or Vimeo links**
- A descriptive title for each video

**Tip: titles that say what it shows**

A title such as “How Our Bogie Drop Table Speeds Up Wheelset Changes” tells readers what they will see. Avoid titles such as “Video 1”.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Can I add a video to my magazine pages?

Yes. Send a YouTube or Vimeo link with your magazine content. It takes the space of about 200 words on the page.

Where do I send my video links?

Please send everything to our **Content Team**. For large files, use a transfer service such as WeTransfer, Dropbox or Google Drive.
