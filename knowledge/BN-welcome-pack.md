## Getting Started

**Important:** To make sure our emails reach you, please add all **@a2bglobalmedia.com** addresses to your address book or contact list. Not sure you are receiving our emails? Please get in touch.

You build and update your company page yourself, in **Manage My Page** on your Bus-News account.

Watch our [**Onboarding Guide**](https://a2b-global-media-content-team.github.io/client-resources/BN-onboarding-guide.html) for a step-by-step tour, from your activation email to your published page.

1. **Activate your account** We email you an invitation with the subject “Activate your account to manage *your company*”. Click the link within a week. You are signed straight in.
2. **Keep your login details** A second email, “Your login details for *your company*”, gives your username and password. Keep it for when you next sign in. If your page appears on more than one of our sites, the same login works on each.
3. **Build your page** Go to **My Account › Manage My Page** and work through the sections: Company details, Company imagery, Company profile, Company categories and Company events. Click the Save button at the end of each section before you open the next one. Your page stays a private draft until it is published.
4. **Submit for publication** When the checklist below is complete, click **Submit for publication**. Our Content Team reviews your page and publishes it.

### Before you can submit

- Company name
- Contact email
- Website URL
- Telephone
- Address
- Region
- Logo
- At least one hero image
- Company profile of at least 300 words
- At least one category

Your page stays editable after it is published, so you can keep improving it. If you are on a Premium or Ultra package, we also book an onboarding call with you.

Some things are not in Manage My Page yet: videos, documents, press releases, banners and magazine content. Send these to the Content Team. For large files, use a transfer service such as:

**WeTransfer** Large files, no account needed

**Dropbox** Share a folder or file link

**Google Drive** Share a folder or file link

Having technical or other difficulties? Contact the Content Team.

### Profile Text

**300–1,000 words.** You write your profile, and we take care of the technical side, such as the page address, meta description and other search settings. A profile strength meter shows your word count and ticks off a heading, paragraphs, a link and a list as you add them. You need 300 words to submit, and the meter reaches full strength at 1,000.

Search engines, and AI tools such as ChatGPT and Google’s AI Overviews, read your profile to decide when to show it or quote it. These eight tips help them understand what you do, and help your readers too.

**Important:** Your content can be similar to what already exists elsewhere on the web, but please do not copy it word for word. Search engines penalise copied content.

1. **Start with a summary that stands on its own** Open with two or three sentences that say who you are, what you supply, who to and where. AI tools often quote a page’s opening sentences, so they need to make sense on their own.
2. **Give every section a heading that says what it is about** Your profile is one text box. To make a heading, click in the line and choose **Heading** from the **Paragraph** menu in the toolbar (choose **Subheading** for a smaller one). Each heading appears as a main heading on your page, and search engines rely on these headings to understand it. Avoid “About Us” or “Our Products”. You can also write a heading as a question your buyers ask, such as “How does Company ABC cut depot maintenance time?”
3. **Plan your sections**
  - **Overview:** the heading includes your company name and what you do, for example “Company ABC – Passenger Information Systems for Buses”.
  - **Applications:** one section for each application you work in, for example “Bus Depot Charging Infrastructure” (not just “Charging”) or “Electric Bus Batteries”. Describe the products you supply for each one.
  - **Case studies:** give each one a precise heading, such as “Case Study: Depot Charging for a 100-Bus Electric Fleet”, then set out the challenge, what you supplied, and the result, with a number if you can.
4. **Keep to one topic per section, in short paragraphs** Aim for two to four sentences per paragraph. Search engines and AI tools often pick out a single passage rather than the whole page, so each section should make sense if read on its own.
5. **Use lists for anything that is naturally a list** Products, services, sectors, technical specifications and certifications are all easier to scan as a bulleted list, and AI tools reuse lists readily.
6. **Give facts, not claims** Name the standards you meet (for example UN ECE R107 or ISO 9001), the projects and customers you can mention, and figures such as fleet numbers, countries or years in business. Words like “world-leading” and “innovative” add nothing unless a fact backs them up.
7. **Use the words your customers search for, and use them consistently** Be precise rather than vague: “bus passenger counting software” is better than just “software”. Write your company name exactly as it appears in Company details, and spell product names the same way every time, so search engines recognise them.
8. **Link to your own pages with descriptive link text** Link to the relevant product pages or case studies on your website. Use words that describe the page, such as “our electric bus case studies”, rather than “click here”.

#### Weak and strong examples

|  | Weak | Strong |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Opening sentence** | We are a leading provider of innovative solutions. | Company ABC is a UK manufacturer of passenger information displays for buses, supplying operators and bus manufacturers across Europe. |
| **Heading** | Our Products | Passenger Information Displays for Buses |
| **Claim** | World-leading quality | Fitted to over 5,000 buses in 25 countries |
| **Link text** | Click here | See our electric bus case studies |

#### Before you submit, check that your profile has

- An opening summary that stands on its own
- A clear heading on every section
- One topic per section, in short paragraphs
- At least one list
- Specific facts and figures
- A case study, if you have one
- A link to your own website
- No text copied word for word
