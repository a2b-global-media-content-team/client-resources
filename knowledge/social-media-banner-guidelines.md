# Social Media Banner Posts: Client Guidelines

> What we need from a client for one feature. Applies across all our sites. Text copied automatically from the [web page](https://a2b-global-media-content-team.github.io/client-resources/social-media-banner-guidelines.html); that page is the master version.

Social Media Banner Posts

## Your banner on *LinkedIn*

A post with only your content, shared with the followers of our LinkedIn page. This page explains what we need from you, and when.

## What You Get

|  | Essentials | Essentials+ | Premium | Ultra |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Social media banner posts | — | 2 | 4 | 4 |

We post your banner on the LinkedIn page of the site your package is on, with your text and a link to your website. We tag your company page, so readers can click straight through to you.

**1024 × 512 px** Banner size

**JPEG, PNG, GIF or PDF** File format

**LinkedIn** Channel

## What We Need From You

- Your banner, 1024 × 512 px
- The URL the post should link to
- Your preferred post text
- Your preferred hashtags
- Your LinkedIn company page, so we can tag you
- Your preferred posting date

**Tip: keep the banner simple**

One clear message and one call to action work best. LinkedIn shows the banner small on phones, so keep text on the image short and large.

## Booking and Timing

You can book your posts in advance with the booking form in your Client Dashboard. Booking a post sends you a reminder to supply your assets.

On booking

#### Posting date agreed

Book as far ahead as you like. We send you a reminder to supply your banner and text.

At least 1 week before the posting date

#### Assets received

We need a week to prepare your post and get your approval. Short notice is possible, but a week lets us do it properly.

Before the posting date

#### You approve the post

We send you the post for approval. We do not publish it until you approve it.

Posting date

#### Your post goes live

We publish your post on our LinkedIn page.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Can you design the banner for us?

Yes. Our design team can design your banners from your images, logo and text, and a short brief if you have one. Design is included in Ultra packages (one 300 × 250 px and one 1024 × 512 px banner) and is available as an additional service for other packages. Ask your Partnership Manager for details.

Can you boost my post to reach more people?

Yes. Boosted LinkedIn campaigns are available as an additional service: we boost your post to an audience chosen by industry, location and job role, and report on the results. Ask your Partnership Manager.

Will I see how my post performed?

Yes. Views, interactions and clicks are included in your quarterly stats report.

Do you post my videos or news on LinkedIn too?

We share your press releases on our LinkedIn page. Your videos feature on your profile, our homepage and our newsletter, not on social media.

Where do I send my assets?

Please send everything to our **Content Team**. For large files, use a transfer service such as WeTransfer, Dropbox or Google Drive.
