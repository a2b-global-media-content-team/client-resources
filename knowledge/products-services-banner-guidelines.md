Products & Services Banners

## Your banner where buyers *search*

A banner at the top of the Products & Services pages of your choice, where buyers browse suppliers by category.

## What You Get

|  | Essentials | Essentials+ | Premium | Ultra |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Products & Services page banners | — | 1 page | 2 pages | 4 pages |

Your banner stays live on the pages you choose **for your whole contract**, with a link to your website.

**300 × 250 px** Banner size

**JPEG, PNG or GIF** File format

## What We Need From You

- Your banner, 300 × 250 px
- The URL the banner should link to
- The Products & Services pages (categories) you want it on

**Tip: choose your pages**

Pick the categories your buyers browse most. Your banner works hardest on the pages where your company is also listed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Is this the same as a featured supplier position?

No. The banner is an advert at the top of the page. A [featured supplier position](https://a2b-global-media-content-team.github.io/client-resources/featured-supplier-guidelines.html) lists your company above the other suppliers on the page.

Can I use the same banner in the newsletter?

Yes. Your newsletter banner and your Products & Services banner are the same size (300 × 250 px), so one banner can do both.

Can you design the banner for us?

Yes. Our design team can design your banners from your images, logo and text, and a short brief if you have one. Design is included in Ultra packages (one 300 × 250 px and one 1024 × 512 px banner) and is available as an additional service for other packages. Ask your Partnership Manager for details.

Will I see how my banner performed?

Yes. Banner views and clicks are included in your quarterly stats report.

Where do I send my banner?

Please send everything to our **Content Team**. For large files, use a transfer service such as WeTransfer, Dropbox or Google Drive.
