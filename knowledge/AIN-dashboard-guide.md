# Dashboard Guide: Aviation Industry-News

> How to read the client dashboard. The screens use an example dashboard with dummy figures. Text copied automatically from the [web page](https://a2b-global-media-content-team.github.io/client-resources/AIN-dashboard-guide.html); that page is the master version.

Your dashboard shows what your package includes, what you have used and what is still to come. Watch the tour, then read each page below.

## 1. Your dashboard

Example shown: [Your dashboard (example), page 1](https://a2b-global-media-content-team.github.io/client-resources/AIN-example-dashboard.html#page-1)

Watch this part of the tour: https://a2b-global-media-content-team.github.io/client-resources/AIN-dashboard-guide.html#your-dashboard

Every client has their own online dashboard. It shows what your package includes, what you have used, what is booked and what is still to come, with the detail behind every item.

1. The **Content Team** sends you the link: once your profile is live if you are a new client, or at the start of each new subscription period if you are renewing. Bookmark it.
2. At the top you see your **package** and your **contract runtime**.
3. Use the buttons for your documents, our resources, to email us or to subscribe to our newsletter.
4. Check the **contact details** we have for you: who we send materials to, the email address that gets your enquiries, and the news page we track for you.
5. See the dates of your **quarterly review and renewal calls** with your Partnership Manager.
6. Under **What would you like to do?** you can book features, submit content and book a call with your Partnership Manager or content writer.

**Move between pages** with the page list on the left, or with the Home, Previous, Next and “More” buttons on the dashboard. The date at the bottom of each page shows when the figures were last updated.

### Tour steps

1. **Your client dashboard**: Every client gets their own dashboard. The Content Team sends you the link once your profile is live, or at the start of each new subscription period. This example is filled with dummy figures for a made-up client.

2. **Your package and contract**: At the top you see your package and your contract runtime: the start and end dates of your current subscription.

3. **Quick links**: Use these buttons to open your documents and our resources, to email us, or to subscribe to our newsletter.

4. **Your contact details**: The people we send materials to, the email address that receives enquiries from your profile, and the news page we track for your press releases. Tell the Content Team if anything changes.

5. **Percentage used**: How much of your package you have used so far in this contract period.

6. **Your calls**: The dates of your quarterly review calls and your renewal call with your Partnership Manager, and whether each call is accepted or still to be confirmed.

7. **Extras and notes**: Anything added to your package, such as an extra targeted mail. Notes from your Partnership Manager show under Client Notes, on the right.

8. **What would you like to do?**: Book in a targeted mail, social media banner post or homepage banner, view your profile, submit content and assets, or book a call with your Partnership Manager or content writer.

## 2. Your package at a glance

Example shown: [Your dashboard (example), page 1](https://a2b-global-media-content-team.github.io/client-resources/AIN-example-dashboard.html#page-1)

Watch this part of the tour: https://a2b-global-media-content-team.github.io/client-resources/AIN-dashboard-guide.html#your-package-at-a-glance

The lower half of the first page counts every feature in your package for your contract period.

1. **In contract** is what your package includes, **Used** is what we have delivered, and **Booked** is what is scheduled.
2. **Remaining** is what is still to use. A red box means you still have some left. A green box means you have used it all.
3. **N/A** means it cannot be booked. For example, Products & Services positions run all year, so there is nothing to book.
4. The two boxes on the right count your news, PDFs and videos, and your magazine, content writing and interviews.
5. Use **Page 2: More**, **Page 3: More** and **Page 4: More** to see the details.

**Something still to use?** Book it with the buttons under “What would you like to do?”, or ask the Content Team.

### Tour steps

1. **Your package at a glance**: Every feature in your package, with what is in your contract, what you have used, what is booked and what is left.

2. **In contract, used and booked**: In contract is what your package includes. Used is what we have delivered. Booked is what is already scheduled.

3. **What is left**: Remaining shows what you still have to use. Red means you still have some left, so book it in. Green means you have used it all. N/A means it cannot be booked.

4. **More detail**: Page 2 shows every banner and targeted mail in detail.

5. **Your profile content**: How many news items, PDFs and videos are on your profile. Page 4 lists them all.

6. **Your other dashboards**: If your company is on more than one of our sites, you have a dashboard for each. Click a circle to open it. In this example they link to the Railway, Future and Bus examples.

7. **Magazine, content writing and interviews**: Your magazine appearances, content writing and interviews. Page 3 has the details.

8. **Features carried over**: If you carried features over from a previous contract, they may show as used here. You still get your full package for this contract period.

## 3. Banners and targeted mail

Example shown: [Your dashboard (example), page 2](https://a2b-global-media-content-team.github.io/client-resources/AIN-example-dashboard.html#page-2)

Watch this part of the tour: https://a2b-global-media-content-team.github.io/client-resources/AIN-dashboard-guide.html#banners-and-targeted-mail

Page 2 shows every banner and targeted mail in detail.

1. Each feature has its own section. On the right you see what is **in your package**, what is **used** and what is **booked**.
2. The tables list each item we have run for you, with the date and links, so you can open the newsletter, LinkedIn post or campaign.
3. **Your banner bookings** show the newsletter banners that are scheduled, with the date and the banner.
4. Your **Products & Services banners** and **featured supplier positions** list the category pages they appear on.

### Tour steps

1. **Banners and targeted mail**: Page 2 shows every banner and targeted mail in detail. Use Home and Next to move between pages.

2. **Targeted mails**: Every targeted mail we have sent for you, with the date and a link to the campaign. Bookings show on the right.

3. **In package, used, booked**: Each section shows what is in your package, what is used and what is booked.

4. **Social media banners**: Each social media banner post: the link on your banner and the LinkedIn post.

5. **Homepage banner**: Your homepage banner, as it appears on our homepage.

6. **Newsletter banners**: Every newsletter your banner appeared in, with the date sent, your banner link and the newsletter.

7. **Your banner bookings**: The newsletter banners that are scheduled, with the date and the banner we will use.

8. **Products & Services banners**: Your banner and the Products & Services pages it appears on, with the link it opens.

9. **News pages banner**: Your banner on our news pages.

10. **Featured supplier positions**: The Products & Services pages where your company is a featured supplier.

11. **Graphic design**: Any graphic design we have done for you, if your package includes it.

## 4. Magazines and content writing

Example shown: [Your dashboard (example), page 3](https://a2b-global-media-content-team.github.io/client-resources/AIN-example-dashboard.html#page-3)

Watch this part of the tour: https://a2b-global-media-content-team.github.io/client-resources/AIN-dashboard-guide.html#magazines-and-content-writing

Page 3 shows your magazine content, content writing and interviews.

1. The **Digital Magazine Calendar** lists the Aviation Industry-News magazine issues during your contract period, with the content deadline and publication date. We publish one issue a year.
2. Use **Submit Magazine Content** to send us your magazine content.
3. **Your magazine appearances** list the issues you have been in. **Booked into** shows the issues you are booked into next, and their status.
4. Your **content writing** lists the articles we have written for you, and **interviews** lists any interviews.

### Tour steps

1. **Magazines and content writing**: Page 3 shows your magazine content, content writing and interviews.

2. **Digital Magazine Calendar**: The Aviation Industry-News magazine issues during your contract period, with each content deadline, publication date and event. We publish one issue a year.

3. **Submit magazine content**: Use this button to send us your magazine content before the deadline.

4. **Your magazines**: The issues you have appeared in, and the upcoming issues you are booked into with their status.

5. **Inside front cover**: If your package includes the inside front cover, the issue it is in.

6. **Content writing**: The articles our content writer has written for you, if your package includes content writing.

7. **Interviews**: Any interviews we have done with you.

## 5. Your profile

Example shown: [Your dashboard (example), page 4](https://a2b-global-media-content-team.github.io/client-resources/AIN-example-dashboard.html#page-4)

Watch this part of the tour: https://a2b-global-media-content-team.github.io/client-resources/AIN-dashboard-guide.html#your-profile

Page 4 shows everything on your company profile.

1. **Your supplier news activity feed** lists every news item on your profile, with the publication date. Click a title to open it.
2. **Your documents** and **your videos** list the PDFs and videos on your profile.
3. **All your Products & Services categories** lists the category pages your company is listed on, with a count.
4. Use the **Your Profile** button to open your company page.

### Tour steps

1. **Your news**: Every news item on your profile, newest first, with the publication date. Click a title to open it.

2. **Open your profile**: This button opens your company page on our site.

3. **Your documents and videos**: The PDFs and videos on your profile. Send new ones to the Content Team.

4. **Your categories**: Every Products & Services category your company is listed in, with a count. You choose these in Manage My Page.

2. **Your news on social media**: Each of your news items we shared on our LinkedIn page, with the date and a link.
