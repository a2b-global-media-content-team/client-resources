Content Writing

## Your articles, written by *us*

Four long-form articles a year, written for you by your own dedicated content writer and published on our site and in our newsletter.

## What You Get

|  | Essentials | Essentials+ | Premium | Ultra |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Content writing | — | — | 4 articles | 4 articles |

At the start of your subscription, we introduce you to your content writer, who then gets in touch with you to plan and write your articles.

## How It Works

Step 01

#### Plan

Your writer plans your articles with you, so each one does a different job: explain a technology, document a project, take a position, or introduce a product.

Step 02

#### Material or interview

Each article starts from material you already have, such as white papers or technical papers, or from an interview with you or one of your experts.

Step 03

#### Check and publish

Your writer sends you the draft to check. Then every article is published as a web article on our site and included in our newsletter.

## What We Need From You

- A contact for your writer
- Your existing material (white papers, technical papers, case studies)
- Time with your experts for interviews
- Images for each article, in high resolution
- Links to the relevant pages on your website

**Magazine first**

If your package includes the magazine, your content writing is used for your magazine pages first. Those articles appear in the magazine and as web articles. Please make sure your writer has your material or interview time before the content writing booking deadline in your Welcome Pack.
