This document outlines everything you need to know about preparing and approving a targeted mail campaign with a2b Global Media — from supplying assets through to sign-off and send.

1

Assets Required

We require all assets before we can begin work. Please do not submit a partial brief — we will hold the job until everything has been received.

Please supply the following

  • All body copy and text (proofread and final)
  • All images, in high resolution (PNG or JPEG)
  • Logo file(s) — PNG with transparent background preferred
  • Brand colour hex codes (e.g. #E25E7A)
  • All destination URLs for links and calls to action
  • Subject line and preview text
  • Contact details to include (if applicable)
  • Font preferences — email font support is limited; see note below
  • Social media profile links (if to be included)
  • Any additional reference materials or brand guidelines
Note: Email font support is limited

Most email clients — including all versions of Microsoft Outlook — do not support custom or commercial web fonts. We design using web-safe system fonts (Arial, Helvetica, Georgia, Verdana, and similar) and, where the client supports it, Google Fonts. If you have a preferred typeface, please share your brand guidelines and we will apply it where possible. A system font fallback will always be used for clients that do not support custom fonts.

Tip: Brand colours

Please supply colours as hex codes — for example, #E25E7A. Descriptions such as "rose pink" or "duck egg blue" cannot be applied reliably. If you are unsure of your hex codes, refer to your brand guidelines or ask your design team.

2

Our Design Process

We build all targeted mails as hand-coded HTML, designed specifically for email — not web browsers. Email HTML requires specialist knowledge: layout techniques and CSS that work perfectly on a website will frequently break in Outlook, Gmail, or Apple Mail. We account for this throughout.

Step 01

HTML Design

We code your email from scratch using techniques that ensure visual consistency across Outlook, Gmail, Apple Mail, and mobile clients.

Step 02

Deliverability Testing

We run deliverability checks before sending to maximise inbox placement and minimise the risk of your email being flagged as spam.

Step 03

Client Approval

A draft is shared with you for review and sign-off. We will not send the campaign until written approval has been received.

Why does email HTML differ from web HTML?

Email clients such as Outlook use Microsoft Word's rendering engine, which has significant CSS limitations. Other clients (Gmail, Apple Mail, mobile apps) each have their own quirks. We design to handle all of these, so your email looks professional wherever it lands.

3

Supplying Your Own HTML

If you would prefer to supply a fully coded HTML file rather than a brief and assets, we can work with that — subject to the following requirements.

  • Your HTML must be compatible with Mailchimp. We send all campaigns through Mailchimp, and proprietary template tags or syntax from other platforms — including Zoho Campaigns and Microsoft platforms — are not supported and will cause errors.
  • Do not include a footer in your HTML file. We will insert our own footer containing the unsubscribe link, opt-out option, and postal address, which is required for legal compliance and our sending policy. Any footer present in a supplied file will be removed.
  • All links should be plain, absolute URLs — not shortened, tracking-wrapped, or platform-specific redirect links.
  • Images must be hosted at a publicly accessible URL, or supplied to us as separate files for us to host.
  • Please do not include JavaScript, embedded video, forms, or interactive elements — these are not supported in email clients.
Please note

Even where you supply your own HTML, we reserve the right to modify it for Mailchimp compatibility, deliverability, and legal compliance. We will flag any significant changes to you before sending.

5

Approval & Amends

When you receive your draft, please review it carefully before responding. Our standard service includes two rounds of amends.

  • Please consolidate all feedback into a single, clearly written response per round, rather than sending changes in multiple separate messages.
  • Additional rounds of amends beyond the standard two may be subject to an additional charge.
  • Amends arising from our own error — for example, a missing or incorrect link, a typo introduced by us, or a layout issue on our part — will not count against your rounds of amends.
  • The campaign will not be sent without written sign-off from you. Verbal approval is not sufficient.
Please check carefully before approving

Before giving approval, please verify all links, all text, all images, and any contact details or dates. We recommend that all relevant internal stakeholders review the draft before you sign off.

6

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if I miss the two-week asset deadline?
Late submission of assets may mean your send date needs to be rescheduled. Please contact us as early as possible if you anticipate a delay. We will do our best to accommodate you, but cannot guarantee the original send date if assets are received late.
What image formats and sizes do you need?
We accept JPEG and PNG files. Please supply images at the highest resolution available — we will crop, resize, and optimise them for email. For logos, PNG files with a transparent background are strongly preferred. Do not embed images inside a Word or PDF document, as they will be too low resolution to use.
Can I supply a PDF or Word document with my copy and layout ideas?
Yes — we can work from a PDF or Word document for copy and layout reference. However, all images and logos must still be supplied as separate files, and all links must be provided as full URLs in a separate document or email.
Which email clients do you test against?
We test across the major email environments, including Microsoft Outlook (multiple versions), Gmail (web and mobile), Apple Mail, and mobile clients on iOS and Android. Our goal is a consistent, professional appearance across all major platforms.
Will my email look identical across all email clients?
Not always — and this is normal in email design. Some clients, particularly older versions of Outlook, have significant rendering limitations. We design emails to degrade gracefully, so the result will always look professional, but minor differences in fonts, spacing, or layout between clients are inherent to email HTML.
Can I include video in my email?
Embedded video is not reliably supported across email clients. The best approach — and the one we recommend — is a still image with a play button overlay, linking through to your video on YouTube, Vimeo, or your website. We are happy to set this up for you.
Do you provide copywriting or translation services?
We are happy to advise on layout, structure, and email best practice, but all copy must be supplied by you — proofread, approved internally, and ready to use. Copywriting and translation are outside our standard service scope.
Will I receive campaign performance data after the send?
Yes. Campaign metrics — including open rate, click rate, and other key data — are included in our quarterly stats report, which is shared with all clients.
Can I make changes after I have approved the email?
If you need to request a change after approval, please contact us immediately. If there is sufficient time before the scheduled send date, we will do our best to accommodate the amendment. If there is not enough time, we will pause the campaign and wait to hear from you before proceeding. Please note that a sent email cannot be recalled or amended, so we strongly encourage all stakeholders to review the draft thoroughly before giving sign-off.
Where do I send my assets?
Please send all assets to content@a2bglobalmedia.com. If helpful, include a brief note with your booking reference or company name so we can assign your campaign correctly.
