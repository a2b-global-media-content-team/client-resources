# Featured Supplier Positions: Client Guidelines

> What we need from a client for one feature. Applies across all our sites. Text copied automatically from the [web page](https://a2b-global-media-content-team.github.io/client-resources/featured-supplier-guidelines.html); that page is the master version.

Featured Supplier Positions

## Top of the list on Products & *Services*

Your company listed above all other suppliers on the Products & Services pages you choose, with a link to your company profile.

## What You Get

|  | Essentials | Essentials+ | Premium | Ultra |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Featured supplier positions | — | Up to 2 | Up to 4 | Up to 8 |

On each page you choose, your company shows at the top of the page, above the other suppliers, with your logo, a short description and a link to your company profile.

## How to Choose Your Positions

Step 01

#### Add your categories

In Manage My Page, open Company categories and choose the categories your company is listed in (up to 20).

Step 02

#### Pick your featured ones

Open Featured categories and choose the categories where you want to show at the top. You can feature up to your package limit.

Step 03

#### Save

Click Save featured categories. You can change your choice at any time in Manage My Page.

**Your profile does the work**

Readers who click your featured listing land on your company profile, so keep your profile text, logo and images up to date.

## What We Need From You

- Your categories, chosen in Manage My Page
- Your featured categories, chosen in Manage My Page
- An up-to-date company profile and logo

## Frequently Asked Questions

Can I feature a category I am not listed in?

Featured categories are chosen from your own categories. Add the category under Company categories first.

Where does the short description come from?

We write it for your company profile. It is the same short description that search engines show for your profile. Ask the Content Team if you would like it changed.

Is this the same as a Products & Services banner?

No. A featured position lists your company above the other suppliers. A [Products & Services banner](https://a2b-global-media-content-team.github.io/client-resources/products-services-banner-guidelines.html) is an advert at the top of the page.

I cannot see Featured categories in Manage My Page.

Featured categories only shows if your package includes featured supplier positions (Essentials+, Premium and Ultra). If you think it is missing, contact the Content Team.
