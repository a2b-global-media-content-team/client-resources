## Typical Enquiries

Readers contact suppliers directly through the enquiry form on their company profile. Here are some of the enquiries our suppliers have received.

Enquiry: We have a tender that requires your product. Please provide an offer for the required product as soon as possible and I’ll get back to you with mandatory technical specifications of thermal containers used for vehicle inspection on the road.

Enquiry: We are interested in your docking solution. I would like to know more about your partners in Australia.

Enquiry: I would like to enquire about whether you can provide a CAN symbol file (either peak sym or dbc) for translating the messages transmitted by the G20-VA05 REV D.

Enquiry: We currently use Tritium Chargers at our current EV Depot but want to put wireless charging pads in to the other EV depots due to room. Please send information on your charging pads.
