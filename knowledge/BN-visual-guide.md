4. **Featured Suppliers**: The homepage featured supplier position puts your banner advert in the Featured Suppliers section, with a link to your website.

## 2. Company Profile

Example shown: [bus-news.com/suppliers/elydium-data-quality-solutions-for-passenger-transport](https://bus-news.com/suppliers/elydium-data-quality-solutions-for-passenger-transport)

Watch this part of the tour: https://a2b-global-media-content-team.github.io/client-resources/BN-visual-guide.html#company-profile

**Hero image**: A showcase image behind your company name.

**Profile text**: 300+ words, written with key phrases that help search engines send traffic to your page.

**Logo, links and contact details**: Your logo, website and social media links, address and telephone number.

**Events**: The events you are attending, with dates and stand numbers. Your next event also shows beside your enquiry form.

**News, videos, documents and images**: Your own news feed, video gallery, downloadable PDFs and image gallery. Add new content at any time.

**Enquiry form**: Readers can contact you directly from your profile.

### Tour steps

1. **Your company profile**: Your profile is your home on Bus-News. It brings together everything about your company in one place, and it is where readers land from your news, banners and listings.

2. **Hero image**: A large showcase image runs behind your company name at the top of the page. Choose an image that shows your products or people at their best. (Landscape image; High resolution)

3. **Logo, links and contact details**: Your logo sits beside your profile, with links to your website and social media, your address and a telephone button. Readers can also save you as a favourite.

4. **Profile text**: 300+ words about your company, enriched with key phrases so search engines send relevant traffic to your profile. You can add your products and services too. (300+ words)

5. **Latest news, right beside your profile**: Your most recent news items show in the sidebar, with a link to all your supplier news.

6. **Events you are attending**: Add the events you are attending, with the date and your stand number, so readers know where to meet you in person. (Event dates; Stand numbers)

7. **News feed**: Every press release you send us appears in your own news feed. Your news also features on the homepage, in our newsletter and across our social channels.

8. **Documents and brochures**: Host brochures, case studies and white papers as PDFs on your profile. Each document is optimised for search engines, so buyers can find it. You can host videos on your profile too. (PDF; Videos)

9. **Image gallery**: Add as many images as you like. Captions help search engines find your images. (Unlimited images)

10. **Enquiry form**: Readers can contact you directly with the enquiry form at the bottom of your profile. Enquiries go straight to your inbox.

11. **Where to meet you**: Your next event shows beside the enquiry form too, so readers who want to talk to you can also find you on your stand.

## 6. Targeted Mail

Example shown: [mailchi.mp/bus-news.com/do-you-offer-transport-for-schools-or-staff-we-can-help-you](https://mailchi.mp/bus-news.com/do-you-offer-transport-for-schools-or-staff-we-can-help-you)

Watch this part of the tour: https://a2b-global-media-content-team.github.io/client-resources/BN-visual-guide.html#targeted-mail

An email with only your content, sent to your most relevant audience, chosen by our AI software, with optional parameters (for example, region) from you.

**Process**: Send your content one week before the agreed send date. We send you a preview to approve, then send your email at the agreed date and time.

**We need**: A subject line (about 65 characters), the content (text and images, as PDF or HTML), valid links, and your preferred audience parameters.

**Design service**: We can design your campaign for you. Ask us for details.

### Tour steps

1. **AI-targeted mail**: An email campaign with only your content: press releases, videos, brochures, webinars or event invitations.

2. **Sent by Bus-News**: Your email is sent from Bus-News, a sender our readers know and trust, which helps it reach the inbox and get opened.

3. **Your content, your audience**: Your mail goes to your most relevant audience, chosen by our AI software, with optional parameters (for example, region) from you.

4. **Your call to action**: Each mail leads to a clear next step, such as a link to your website, a demo request or your stand at an event.

5. **How it works**: - Send your content one week before the agreed send date
- We send you a preview to approve
- We send your email at the agreed date and time (Subject line ≈ 65 characters; PDF or HTML; Valid links)
