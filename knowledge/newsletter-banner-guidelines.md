# Newsletter Banners: Client Guidelines

> What we need from a client for one feature. Applies across all our sites. Text copied automatically from the [web page](https://a2b-global-media-content-team.github.io/client-resources/newsletter-banner-guidelines.html); that page is the master version.

Newsletter Banners

## Your banner in our *newsletter*

A 300 × 250 px banner in our newsletter, linking to your website, in front of our subscribers in your sector.

## What We Need From You

- Your banner, 300 × 250 px
- The URL the banner should link to
- Any dates or months you would like, or avoid

The same 300 × 250 px banner can also be your Products & Services banner. If you do not send a separate newsletter banner, we use your Products & Services banner.

**Animated GIFs in email**

Some email programs, including older versions of Outlook, show only the first frame of an animated GIF. Please put your key message in the first frame.

## Booking and Timing

Start of your contract

#### Send your banners

Please send your banners at the start of your contract, so we can book them into your quarterly, every-other-month or monthly slots.

Through your contract

#### We schedule your slots

We spread your banners evenly across your contract and include them in the newsletter on those dates.

During your contract

#### Switch a banner if you need to

You can switch a banner for a later slot, for example to promote an event. Send the new banner to the Content Team as early as you can before that slot.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use the same banner every time?

Yes. You can send one banner for all your slots, or a different one for each. We still ask you to send your banners at the start, so we can book all your slots in.

Can you design the banner for us?

Yes. Our design team can design your banners from your images, logo and text, and a short brief if you have one. Design is included in Ultra packages (one 300 × 250 px and one 1024 × 512 px banner) and is available as an additional service for other packages. Ask your Partnership Manager for details.

Will I see how my banner performed?

Yes. Banner views and clicks are included in your quarterly stats report.

Where do I send my banners?

Please send everything to our **Content Team**. For large files, use a transfer service such as WeTransfer, Dropbox or Google Drive.
