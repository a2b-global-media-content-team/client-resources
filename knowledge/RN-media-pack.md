## Typical Enquiries

Readers contact suppliers directly through the enquiry form on their company profile. Here are some of the enquiries our suppliers have received.

Enquiry: I would like to test your wheel measuring scanner if possible to validate it for company wide use at over 80 rail operations across the USA.

Enquiry: I would like a quote and lead time on 4x carriage lifting jacks at 15 tonnes each (approx.)? I can provide the lifting arm drawings as well as the height dimension restrictions, through email.

Enquiry: We were looking at an alternative suggestion for Cloud 9 flame retardant underlay. We would require a product that meets EN45545-2 HL2 at R1 standards?

Enquiry: I am looking to complete a pedestrian capacity assessment survey for Leicester Station and would like to understand if this is something that your organisation would be interested in quoting for?
