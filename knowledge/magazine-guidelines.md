Digital Magazine

## Your pages in our *magazine*

Up to three A4 pages in our interactive e-magazine, split between adverts and advertorial as you choose, and published as a PDF on your profile too.

Each issue gives you **three A4 pages**. A popular choice is a one-page advert with a two-page advertorial. Your pages also appear as a stand-alone PDF on your company profile, so readers can download them.

## Sizes and Specifications

**210 × 297 mm** Full page · 2480 × 3508 px

**420 × 297 mm** Double page · 4961 × 3508 px

**190 × 131 mm** Half page · 2244 × 1547 px

All adverts at **300 dpi**.

### For an advertorial, please supply

- 300–400 words per page, final text only, in an editable format such as Word
- 1 high-resolution image per page (tell us the order if you send more)
- Your company logo
- Contact details, usually for a salesperson
- Optional: a YouTube or Vimeo link *(takes the space of about 200 words)*
- Optional: details for a QR code

### For an advert, please supply

- Your designed advert, final version
- At the sizes above, 300 dpi

## Frequently Asked Questions

Can you write my advertorial?

Yes. If your package includes [content writing](https://a2b-global-media-content-team.github.io/client-resources/content-writing-guidelines.html), it is used for your magazine pages first. Content writing is also available as an additional service.

Can you design my advert?

Yes. Design of one A4 advert is included in Ultra packages, and available as an additional service for other packages.

Can I add a video?

Yes. Send a YouTube or Vimeo link. It takes the space of about 200 words on the page.

What if I miss the content deadline?

Please contact us as early as possible. We may not be able to include your pages in that issue.
