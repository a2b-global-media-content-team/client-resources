# Documents: Client Guidelines

> What we need from a client for one feature. Applies across all our sites. Text copied automatically from the [web page](https://a2b-global-media-content-team.github.io/client-resources/document-guidelines.html); that page is the master version.

Documents

## Your documents on your *profile*

We host your white papers, brochures, case studies and datasheets as PDFs on your company profile, where readers can download them.

## What You Get

- Your PDFs in the Documents section of your company profile
- Each document optimised for search engines, so buyers can find it
- Your magazine pages added as a PDF too, if your package includes the magazine

## What We Need From You

- Your document as a PDF
- A clear, descriptive title for each document

**Tip: name your documents clearly**

A title such as “Track Inspection System: Technical Datasheet” tells readers, and search engines, what they will get. Avoid file names such as “brochure_v3_final”.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Can I replace a document with a new version?

Yes. Send the new version to the Content Team and tell us which document it replaces.

Where do I send my documents?

Please send everything to our **Content Team**. For large files, use a transfer service such as WeTransfer, Dropbox or Google Drive.
