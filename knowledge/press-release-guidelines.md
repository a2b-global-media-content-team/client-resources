Supplier Press Releases

## Your news, on our *channels*

We publish your press releases on your profile, our homepage and our supplier news page, include them in our newsletter and share them on our LinkedIn page.

## How Your News Reaches Us

Step 01

#### Automatic news tracking

Essentials+, Premium and Ultra: add the address of your news page in Manage My Page › Company details. We pick up each new press release from your website, so you do not need to send it in.

Step 02

#### Email

If you prefer not to opt in, or on Essentials, email each press release to the Content Team.

Step 03

#### Publish

As standard, we publish your news straight away. If you would like to approve it first, ask the Content Team: it then appears in the For Review tab of your account.

## What We Need From You

If we track your news automatically, we take your press release and image from your website, so you do not need to send anything. If you email your news, please send:

- The press release text, in English
- A headline
- At least one image, in high resolution
- Image credit or caption, if needed
- Links to the relevant pages on your website
- A contact for media enquiries, if you have one

**Tip: write for search**

A clear headline with your company name and what is new, and a first paragraph that says who, what, where and when, help search engines and AI tools find your news.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Do you edit my press release?

No. We publish your press release as you send it, so please check it carefully before you send it.

Can I send news in another language?

Please send your news in English.

Do I need to send an image?

Yes. Every press release needs at least one image.

Where do I send my news?

Please send everything to our **Content Team**. For large files, use a transfer service such as WeTransfer, Dropbox or Google Drive.
