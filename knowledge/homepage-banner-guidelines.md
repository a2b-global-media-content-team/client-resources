# Homepage Banners: Client Guidelines

> What we need from a client for one feature. Applies across all our sites. Text copied automatically from the [web page](https://a2b-global-media-content-team.github.io/client-resources/homepage-banner-guidelines.html); that page is the master version.

Homepage Banners

## Your banner on our *homepage*

A banner in the Featured Suppliers section of our homepage, the busiest page on the site, linking to your website.

## What You Get

|  | Essentials | Essentials+ | Premium | Ultra |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Homepage banner | — | 4 weeks, below the fold | 6 months, above the fold | 12 months, above the fold |

**1024 × 512 px** Banner size

**JPEG, PNG or GIF** File format

## What We Need From You

- Your banner, 1024 × 512 px
- The URL the banner should link to
- Your preferred start date (4-week banners)

## Booking and Timing

When we need your banner depends on your package.

Ultra (12 months)

#### Start of your contract

Your banner runs for your whole contract, so please send it at the start of your contract.

Essentials+ (4 weeks)

#### Book your 4 weeks

Book the 4 weeks you want with the booking form in your Client Dashboard. You do not need to book far in advance.

Essentials+ and Premium: 5 working days before the start date

#### Banner received

Send your banner and link at least 5 working days before your banner starts.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change my banner during my contract?

Yes. Send the new banner and link to the Content Team, and we replace it.

Can you design the banner for us?

Yes. Our design team can design your banners from your images, logo and text, and a short brief if you have one. Design is included in Ultra packages (one 300 × 250 px and one 1024 × 512 px banner) and is available as an additional service for other packages. Ask your Partnership Manager for details.

Will I see how my banner performed?

Yes. Banner views and clicks are included in your quarterly stats report.

Where do I send my banner?

Please send everything to our **Content Team**. For large files, use a transfer service such as WeTransfer, Dropbox or Google Drive.
